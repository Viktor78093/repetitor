Вакансии

Школа ищет преподавателей иностранных языков. Описание вакансии — на английском.

The School of Foreign Languages “Repetitor” is looking for professional teachers.

Requirements

  • A degree in Teaching Foreign Languages (English/ Chinese/ Japanese/ French/ German)
  • A High level of Proficiency in written and spoken foreign language
  • Minimum two years of proven teaching experience
  • Knowledge of communicative method of foreign language teaching
  • PC skills
  • Creativity, enthusiasm, motivation and desire to teach and learn

We offer

  • Competitive salary package
  • Career opportunities
  • Continuous learning and development
  • Friendly working environment
